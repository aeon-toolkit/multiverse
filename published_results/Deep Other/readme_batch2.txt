Batch 2 extracted classification tables

Files included
- Tian2025FreRA.csv: Table 1 from FreRA. This is an aggregate results table, covering three HAR datasets plus average UEA and UCR archive results rather than full per-dataset UEA scores.
- Wang2023WHEN.csv: Table 1 classification results on 30 UEA datasets from WHEN.
- Wen2024VQShape.csv: Table 5 full benchmark results on 29 UEA datasets from VQShape.
- Xiao2021RTFN.csv: Table 10 full UEA2018 classification results from RTFN.
- Ren2024RankSCL.csv: Aggregate benchmark results from RankSCL, combining Table I (average ranks on UCR/UEA) and Table II (average ACC/Precision/F1/Recall). This paper does not report a per-dataset UEA results table in the uploaded file.
- Swiderski2025GrowingNN.csv: Table 9 multivariate time-series classification accuracy comparison on 11 datasets from GrowingNN.

Notes
- Dataset abbreviations were expanded to standard archive names where the paper used shorthand, especially in Xiao2021RTFN.csv.
- Missing entries shown as dashes in the papers were kept as blank cells.
- Wen2024VQShape.csv uses the paper's full Table 5 benchmark results, not the smaller summary tables.
- Tian2025FreRA.csv and Ren2024RankSCL.csv are aggregate result tables only, because the uploaded papers do not provide a full per-dataset UEA result table in the main file.
